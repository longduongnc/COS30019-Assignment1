# Topic selection & key issues (Weeks 2–3)

## Chosen topic
**Topic 6 — What is the effectiveness of ethics guidelines and principles in practice?**

### Why this topic
- It is the central question of S2: Hagendorff (2020) concludes that guidelines "most often" have no real impact.
- Australia's own path (2019 principles → 2024 Voluntary AI Safety Standard → 2025 Guidance for AI Adoption) is a live case study of trying to close the gap between principles and practice.
- There is empirical evidence to work with (surveys, interviews and experiments with practitioners), which lets the report go beyond opinion.

## Notes from S2 (Hagendorff, 2020)
- 22 guidelines compared; most commonly covered: accountability, privacy, fairness (each in about 80% of guidelines).
- Ethics "lacks mechanisms to reinforce its own normative claims" (p. 99).
- Industry guidelines can discourage binding law (the self-governance argument).
- §3.3: the McNamara et al. controlled study found no significant effect of the ACM code on decisions.
- Beck's metaphor: ethics is "a bicycle brake on an intercontinental airplane" (p. 108).
- Proposed remedies: move from deontological rule lists toward virtue ethics; add institutional changes such as legal frameworks, audits and whistleblower protection.
